To switch on a channel that isn't listed, or to change its model and budgets, run /proactive on or /configure bot in that channel. A forum can be switched on here as a whole: each of its posts is its own channel, and a post switched off with /proactive off stays off. A forum can also send its new posts straight to the assistant. Normally the watcher reads each message first and wakes the assistant only when it thinks the assistant should look. With this on, a new post's opening message always wakes it. Replies in the post still go through the watcher.
